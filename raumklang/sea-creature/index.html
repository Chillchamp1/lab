Hearing Like a Sea Creature

About this scene

You float in the open sea, eighteen metres down, and hear the way an animal there might. Sound carries for kilometres in water – whales sing from many kilometres away, every call arrives several times via the surface and the seabed and rolls on for a long time. Dolphins pass, the reef crackles in the distance, and once a whale swims right past you.

Best with headphones (left and right the right way round), lying down. The scene loops endlessly without an audible seam. The map shows you from above, looking up the screen; every dot is a sound, exactly where the renderer placed it.
