In a Dinosaur Forest in Autumn Rain

You sit leaning against a tree trunk under the canopy. It is raining; on big leaves above you it drums like rain on a tent, a stream flows past on your right, birds sing in the trees. Far away, plant-eaters call, and sometimes something enormous bellows across the valley; now and then one stomps past breaking branches, and one comes close to browse and sniffs at you.

How to listen: headphones on (left and right the right way round), lie down, close your eyes. The scene loops endlessly without an audible seam.
