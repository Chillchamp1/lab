Elephants at the Waterhole

Night on the savanna. You lie in a tent, a waterhole a few metres in front of you. Crickets chirp, frogs croak at the water's edge, hippos grunt far off. A herd of elephants comes to drink: the deep rumbles they talk with, trunks slurping and splashing water. One stomps past the tent – and once, one comes right up and sniffs at the tent.

How to listen: headphones on (left and right the right way round), lie down, close your eyes. The scene loops endlessly without an audible seam.
