In a Wooden Boat at Sea

About this scene

You lie across an open wooden sailing boat, a good six metres long – on cushions, your head propped up against the side, feet towards the other side, the bow to your left, safely below the gunwale. Gulls circle and dart over you, waves roll by in long lines, water knocks hollow against the planks right behind your head, the boat creaks, and now and then the wind catches the slack sail.

Best with headphones (left and right the right way round), lying down. The scene loops endlessly without an audible seam. The view shows every sound exactly where and when the renderer placed it; distances further away are squeezed so that everything fits.
