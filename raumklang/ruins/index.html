In the Monastery Ruins at Night

You lie on the flagstones in the nave of a ruined monastery. The vault has collapsed; above you, the night sky between high walls. Outside it rains softly, wind whistles through an empty window, drops fall onto the stone, two owls call to each other. Sometimes pigeons burst up from the masonry, a bell tolls far across the valley – and once, singing drifts over from far away.

How to listen: headphones on (left and right the right way round), lie down, close your eyes. The scene loops endlessly without an audible seam.
