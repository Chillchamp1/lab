Blind comparison

You hear two versions of the same passage without knowing which is which, and say which one you prefer. Only at the end do you see what was behind X and Y.

  • Headphones on, your EQ preset for the scenes on, crossfeed and room effects off.
  • Set a comfortable volume in the first round and leave it there. Both versions are equally loud.
  • Switch between X and Y as often as you like; both run in sync, so switching doesn't jump in the piece. Keys: 1/2 or ←/→, space pauses.
  • Can't hear a difference? Then choose “same” – that's a result too.
