Ear measurement

Uses in-ear microphones to measure how your headphones sound at your ears and how sound from different directions arrives at your ears. Everything is calculated in the browser; at the end you save a file and put it in the “Raumklang” Drive folder.

1 · Devices

  1. Connect the Zoom H5 to the PC via USB and select USB → Audio Interface → Stereo Mix on the H5.
  2. Plug the OKM into the 3.5 mm input of the XY capsule and turn on Plug-in Power in the H5 menu. Left mic in the left ear.
  3. Open this page in Chrome or Edge and click “Allow microphone” below.
