Which ears suit you best?

How we hear direction depends on the shape of the outer ear. The sound scenes are rendered with a set of measured ears (an HRTF). The better that set matches your ears, the clearer front, back, above and below become.

You’ll hear short noise bursts from different directions, rendered with six different artificial ears and a touch of reverb, as in a small room. Tap where the sound is coming from. You’ll only see which HRTF was behind each one at the end.

Takes about 6–8 minutes. Ordinary headphones, a comfortable volume, and any built-in spatial audio feature switched off. Ideally in the position you’ll listen in later.

Result

Hits = exactly right (out of 8). Near = off by at most one neighbouring position. F/B errors = front and back confused. Outside = sense of space, 1–5. Circle = 0–2.

The result stays on this device and isn’t sent anywhere. To have it analysed, copy it and paste it into the chat with Claude, or save it as a file.

With 8 trials per HRTF this is a first pointer. A second round with the two best and their neighbours makes it reliable.
